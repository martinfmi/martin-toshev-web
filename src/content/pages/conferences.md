Beyond speaking, I help build conference programs and developer communities — bringing practitioners together around the JVM, open source, and practical software engineering.

## ChurConf

I am involved with [ChurConf](https://churconf.com/), including editions such as Auckland and the upcoming Sydney event (alongside Community Over Code). The goal is a focused, high-signal conference experience with strong speakers and an international community.

## Community Over Code

I also support and promote [Community Over Code](https://communityovercode.org/) — the Apache Software Foundation conference — including the Sydney edition running alongside ChurConf.
