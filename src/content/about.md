Tackling difficult problems and sharing knowledge with other people is what drives me.

I am an IT consultant with nearly two decades of experience in the Java ecosystem — spanning architecture, platform security, messaging, search, and large-scale data processing. I work with teams on hard technical problems, help them ship clearer systems, and teach what I learn along the way.

## Focus areas

- **IT consulting** for JVM-based platforms and enterprise systems
- **Cybersecurity** with a practical focus on the Java security model
- **Conference speaking** and community sessions worldwide
- **Hands-on trainings** for developers and architects
- **Writing** — books, articles, and workshop materials

## Selected work

- Author of [Learning RabbitMQ](https://www.amazon.com/Learning-RabbitMQ-Martin-Toshev/dp/1783984562) (Packt Publishing)
- Contributor to open-source teaching materials such as [java_developer_training](https://github.com/martinfmi/java_developer_training) and [java_security_animated](https://github.com/martinfmi/java_security_animated)
- Regular speaker at JavaLand, JavaZone, Devoxx, Voxxed Days, CodeOne, and many JUGs
- Co-organizer of [ChurConf](https://churconf.com/) editions

## Background

I am based in Sofia, Bulgaria, and studied at Sofia University St. Kliment Ohridski. Over the years I have worked across consulting, product engineering, and community building — including involvement with the Bulgarian Java User Group and international conference programs.
